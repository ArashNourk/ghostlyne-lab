What the number means

A modeled liquidation-barrier probability under standardized assumptions: the chance that Binance's mark price reaches the liquidation level of an isolated-margin position with a 0.5% maintenance margin rate, entered at the UTC open, before that UTC day ends. Your real liquidation level depends on the exchange, your maintenance tier, position size, fees and entry price, so treat these odds as a standardized risk measure, not your exact liquidation price. Forecasts use information up to 23:15 UTC the evening before, and only forecasts verifiably public before their window opens count toward the record. "Vs normal" compares the odds with that market's average over the last 90 days, at 10x. "Max lev." is the highest leverage, up to 20x, whose modeled odds are at or below your risk limit (2%, 5% or 10%; 5% by default), for longs / shorts. A modeled odds level is not a guarantee: at 5% roughly one position in twenty would still be liquidated. "None" means no tested leverage (5x to 20x) meets the threshold that day, and Ghostlyne does not name one. It never names 40x: in testing, the rare days when 40x looked safe were liquidated more often than 5%.

Proof, before you trust it

Walk-forward backtest at 10x, on exactly the information the live model uses (a 23:15 UTC cut) and mark-price liquidation labels. Every forecast and every calibration used only earlier data. When Ghostlyne said a given chance, this is how often it happened.

Live record. Every forecast is written to a hash-chained public ledger, its public availability is verified and timestamped, and it is scored after the day closes. Only forecasts verified public before their window opens enter this table. Nothing is edited after publication.

Every line, and what happened
