    Who it is for

    Independent traders and analysts

    See which drivers, near and far, move ERCOT and Henry Hub, at which horizon, who leads, and when a relationship weakens, reverses or diverges.

    Advisors, brokers and smaller energy firms

    Relationships are labelled for short-term wholesale exposure, spread trading, or forward and procurement decisions, so spot findings are never passed off as contract intelligence.

    Why it can be trusted

    Every claim was registered before testing, checked against known energy relationships, and confirmed on later data. The full record, failures included, is public. Associations are not forecasts or causes, and each one says which it is.
