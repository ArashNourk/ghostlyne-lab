# DAILY1 results withheld pending data licensing

The DAILY1 backtest used Coinbase Exchange market data. Coinbase's Market Data Terms restrict displaying data, charts, analytics or research derived from it to third parties without written consent. Until permission is obtained, or the test is re-run on a licensed provider, the results file and report are kept privately as audit evidence and are not published here.

The pre-registration, the frozen engine code (V1) and the freeze record remain public; they contain no market data.
